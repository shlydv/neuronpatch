# Dataset attribution

`prepared/*.json` and `baseline/*.json` include excerpts and derived samples from
[SQuAD 2.0](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/), by Rajpurkar et al.,
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The derived dataset files retain that license; the repository MIT license does
not override it. Changes: article partitioning, paired sampling, token-length
filtering, model responses and scoring fields. Original IDs, titles and contexts
are retained. Source downloads and hashes are recorded in source_manifest.json.
