# Data provenance

Question IDs, gold answer strings and quoted passage excerpts derive from
[SQuAD 2.0](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The frozen source hash, sample IDs and preparation provenance are recorded in
the parent pilot1_data_manifest.json. Full source passages remain in the local
prepared data rather than being duplicated here.

Generated responses and the Codex assistant's qualitative assessments are
experimental outputs. Those assessments are not independent human labels or
replacement benchmark ground truth. The frozen primary evaluator remains
unchanged.
